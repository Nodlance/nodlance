# Contributor Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in Nodlance a welcoming, inclusive, and harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of experience level, background, or identity.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Assume good intent while discussing technical disagreements.
- Give and receive feedback professionally.
- Focus criticism on ideas and code, not people.
- Respect privacy and avoid publishing personal information without consent.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, personal attacks, discrimination, threats, doxxing, and sustained disruptive behavior are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards. Report abusive behavior privately to the maintainers. The project should publish a dedicated contact method before inviting broad public participation.
